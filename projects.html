Project 1

Lost in the Everglades

This program was coded for my final project in a beginner C++ course. The menu features rules,

the game itself, and a quit option. The user plays as a ranger that must advance through the Everglades

in order to save the tourists that are trapped on the opposite end of the map. The ranger must advance

carefully and avoid the dangerous enemies that may be randomly encountered. They can choose to either fight

the enemies or wait, but they must reach the tourists before the time limit expires.
